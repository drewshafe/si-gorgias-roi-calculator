Methodology & Sources
  • ShipInsure 62% shipping-ticket deflection: Merchants using ShipInsure's 20-second self-service claims portal (98–99% approval rate, 1–2 day resolution) report approximately 62% reduction in shipping-related CS contacts. Source: ShipInsure internal merchant data.
  • Gorgias automation rate — default 30%: Gorgias documentation states: "A good benchmark is a 30% automation rate." The platform-wide median AI Resolution Rate (Oct 2025–Apr 2026 data window) is 45%; top-quartile brands reach 65%. We use 30% as the conservative default for new adopters. Sources: docs.gorgias.com/my-automate · gorgias.com/research
  • Gorgias AHT reduction — default 35%: Published case studies show Good Eggs achieved 40% handle-time reduction and Shinesty saw 50% lower resolution time using Gorgias automation features. We apply 35% as a conservative default for agent-handled tickets. Source: gorgias.com/blog/automation-impact-on-cx-data
  • Gorgias additional published outcomes: 37% reduction in first response time · 52% reduction in resolution time · 27% decrease in ticket-to-order ratio. Source: gorgias.com/blog/automation-impact-on-cx-data
  • Shipping incident rate — default 2%: Industry average for small parcel carriers (lost, damaged, stolen, significantly delayed). [assumption]
  • Ticket-to-order ratio — default 10%: Standard eCommerce CS benchmark; typical observed range 8–12%. [assumption]
  • Shipping-related share of tickets — default 25%: Approximate share of total CS tickets that are shipping-related (WISMO, lost, damaged, disputes). [assumption]
  • Average ticket handle time — default 7 min: Typical end-to-end agent handle time for eCommerce helpdesk tickets across email and chat channels. [assumption]
  • Agent fully-loaded cost — default $25/hr: ~$18–20/hr base wage + ~25–30% benefits, management overhead, and tooling for US-based support. [assumption]
  • SI attach rate — default 60%: ShipInsure merchants using auto-opt-in at checkout typically achieve 50–70% attach rates. Source: ShipInsure platform data.
  • SI premium — default 2.75%: Customer-facing protection fee. Typical ShipInsure range: 2.5–3% of cart. Source: ShipInsure.
  • SI rev share — default 65%: Net rev share range is 50–75%; 65% is mid-range for a standard merchant profile. Source: ShipInsure.
  • Reship cost savings: ShipInsure covers reships at full MSRP/AOV. The merchant absorbs zero cost-of-goods or re-shipping cost for SI-covered incidents. Calculated as: shipping incidents × AOV.
  • Synergy logic (no double-counting): ShipInsure deflects 62% of shipping tickets before they are opened. Gorgias automation then runs on the reduced remaining volume — not the original total. A ticket cannot be deflected by both tools simultaneously. The combined value therefore reflects a genuine compounding effect: SI shrinks the queue, Gorgias optimizes what's left.
